What's inside

Built for the moment you need it.

No logins to remember. No menus to navigate. No points to place manually. It reads your location, tells you what's happening, and shows the way out.

01 / TERRAIN

Global 3D elevation

Real NASA SRTM terrain streamed from AWS Open Data. Rotate, tilt, and read slope and elevation band along any route.

02 / HAZARDS

Live and baseline

ThinkHazard! for static classification. GDACS and NASA EONET for live alerts. Nothing invented, nothing interpolated blindly.

03 / ROUTING

Automatic and honest

Reads your location, finds the nearest source-backed destination, and shows both objectives side by side.

04 / ASSISTANT

Offline survival LLM

A local language model that answers first-aid, water, shelter, and fire questions on-device. No internet required once installed.

05 / PRIVACY

Stays on your machine

Location and accounts live in a local SQLite file. No third-party trackers. No cloud sync. No analytics.

06 / SOURCE

Fully auditable

Every source has a URL, a hash, a licence, and a processing log. The pipeline preserves original bytes.
