CLASSROOM EXPERIMENTS

Simulation lab.

Explore a hypothetical road closure without altering the underlying source data.

Change a condition. Compare the result.

  1. Open the risk map and load a demonstration or choose your endpoints.
  2. Select a road from the route result, or enable the road layer and click a segment.
  3. Apply Blocked, Closed, Flooded or Unavailable to exclude that OSM way from both searches. Unsafe adds a model penalty to the risk-adjusted search.
  4. Review the recalculated comparison. A disconnected route is reported rather than replaced with a fabricated path.
  5. Clear simulated conditions to restore the source graph for your session.
All changes are labelled SIMULATION, stored separately in MySQL and isolated to your browser session. This is not an operational road-closure system.
