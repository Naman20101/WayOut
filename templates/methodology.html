UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

Transparent by design.

WAYOUT is a CBSE Computer Science educational prototype built with Python, MySQL, Flask and a custom Dijkstra algorithm.

From public data to a road graph.

Official sources + OSM15 km cropSpatial joinMySQLDijkstraCompare

Downloads retain source URLs, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes. Roads are clipped to study areas, split at shared OSM junctions and joined to available hazard observations by sampling at approximately 40 m spacing. Mode and one-way tags determine accessible graph edges.

Original source values and a separate normalised score are retained. NoData is missing information, not zero risk. Hospitals and schools are not automatically classified as shelters.

Two objectives. Identical endpoints.

The shortest route minimises graph distance. The risk-adjusted route minimises distance multiplied by a transparent hazard penalty. Both searches use the same snapped junctions, travel mode and simulated exclusions.

effective index = Σ weight × [coverage × score + (1 − coverage) × 100] + Σ terrain_weight × srtm_score
edge cost = distance × [1 + multiplier × effective index / 100 + simulation penalty]

The multiplier is 8 for flood and 5 for earthquake. Tokyo weights are collapse 0.5, fire 0.3 and emergency activity difficulty 0.2. NASA SRTM terrain adds elevation band and slope as an additive modifier with region-specific weights. These are educational assumptions, not calibrated emergency policies. Unsafe simulation adds 20 to the multiplier term; other simulated closures remove the road from both searches.

Dijkstra uses a priority queue and non-negative costs. A least-cost path is optimal only for this graph and objective. It is not proof of safety. Results report both a model index including unknown penalties and an observed index over the supported portion, plus source coverage. Different indices across regions are not directly comparable.

What the evidence supports.

Kochi / Ernakulam

KSDMA’s historical 50-year flood raster supplies a mapped-footprint indicator of 100 where valid. Raw raster units could not be confidently reconciled with the accompanying legend, so WAYOUT makes no depth claims. NoData remains unknown. Because both footprint and unknown penalties are 100, this model cannot distinguish flood severity and may return the same route for both objectives. No operational shelter destination is asserted.

Kathmandu Valley

USGS ShakeMap intensity for the 25 April 2015 Gorkha earthquake is normalised with clamp((MMI − 1) / 9 × 100). This describes one historical event, not a future earthquake prediction. IOM’s historical open-space report is registered as a reference; no unverified point-to-shelter match is used.

Eastern Tokyo

Tokyo Metropolitan Government neighbourhood building-collapse and fire ranks use rank × 20. Emergency activity difficulty uses coefficient × 100, capped at 100. These relative area measures do not diagnose individual buildings. Liquefaction observations and historical polygons are display layers only; point data is not interpolated into street-wide certainty.

Sumida school names are matched exactly against mapped OSM facilities after removing municipality prefixes. Their official 2022 shelter designation is retained, while present opening, capacity and entrance accessibility remain unverified.

Evidence has boundaries.

Road data may be incomplete or outdated. Conditional access, turn restrictions, real-time closures, bridge integrity, evacuation crowding and professional structural inspections are not modelled. The nearest graph junction can be up to 400 m away; the point-to-junction connection is not an assessed access route.

NASA SRTM elevation is a coarse relative-terrain indicator. It cannot substitute for surveyed flood depth, engineering ground assessment or building-level structural review. A low-lying SRTM cell is not a flood forecast.

No building-level structural scores or machine-learning predictions are fabricated. Those tables are intentionally empty because appropriate verified training and inspection data were not obtained. Simulated destinations and conditions are explicitly labelled and stored separately.
